Questions

Is boarding or overnight care included?

No. Yourgi Pack covers walks, and daycare on the Weekdays plan. Overnight stays and house-sitting are booked and paid for the normal way — they're not part of a plan.

What happens to walks I don't use?

PROPOSED, not yet approved — unused walks roll into next month, up to one month's worth. A quiet month doesn't cost you anything; you just can't stockpile them forever.

Can I cancel?

Any month, from the link on your receipt. Your plan runs through the month you've already paid for. PLACEHOLDER — confirm against the live Stripe cancellation behaviour.

Can I change plans?

PLACEHOLDER — confirm upgrade/downgrade timing and whether Stripe prorates the difference.

Do I get the same Pro every time?

That's the point of a plan. We match you to one local Pro and keep them on your days. If they're sick or on holiday we'll tell you who's covering before it happens.

What if I need an extra walk one week?

Book it the normal way and pay for that one walk. Your plan isn't a cap on how much care you can get — it's the part that repeats.
